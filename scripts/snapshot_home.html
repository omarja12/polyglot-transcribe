About & How it works

Polyglot Transcribe converts short audio snippets or uploaded recordings into clean meeting or consultation reports. The app transcribes audio (Whisper models) and then generates a structured report (summary, key points, decisions, follow-ups) using a configurable LLM on Groq. For portfolio demos, the report model is configurable via environment variables so the demo runs with your available account models.
